# Camille's voice

Example of a voice profile, with invented excerpts. The real profile follows this layout; `SKILL.md` says where it lives.

## Traits

- **Starts with the subject.** The first sentence is a fact or a finding, never an announcement of what follows.
- **Short, factual sentences**, one idea per line, no superlatives.
- **Typography:** straight apostrophes ('), no em dash, no bold.

## Never writes

- "Dans cet article, je vais…", "Voici…", any announcement of the content.
- Closing calls: "N'hésitez pas à partager vos commentaires".

## Excerpts

Anonymization rules for any new excerpt: people become a role ("une collègue", "le client"), employers and clients become "mon employeur", "une start-up", places shrink to a city or disappear, dates to the year. Leave out health, family matters and conflicts at work, even anonymized.

### A finding, in a post (2025)

> Pour info, on a divisé par trois le temps de build. Rien de magique : on a arrêté de recompiler ce qui n'avait pas changé.

### A short professional request (2024)

> Bonjour, est-ce que vous pourriez m'envoyer le devis signé d'ici vendredi ? Sinon pas de souci, je décale la commande d'une semaine.
